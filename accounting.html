CASE STUDY

Accounting App - Desktop Web App Design

A desktop accounting application designed to organize financial operations, including ledger management, banking, inventory, sales, and purchases. I worked closely with a client to understand the product requirements and translate them into structured interfaces and connected accounting workflows.

About the Project

The Accounting App is a financial management platform designed to support essential business accounting operations, including ledger management, banking, inventory, sales, purchases, and financial reporting.

I designed the application from scratch, working closely with the client, an accounting professional, to translate accounting requirements into structured interfaces and practical workflows.

The goal was to create a clear and organized accounting experience that makes financial information easier to access, manage, and understand during everyday business operations.

Scope and Constraints

  1. 01 Scope
    1. Product Scope:

      The application was designed to bring essential accounting and business operations into one platform.

      • Financial overview through a dashboard.
      • Ledger and bank book management.
      • Trial balance and financial records.
      • Inventory management, sales and purchase operations.
    2. Design Scope:

      My work covered the product's interface design from initial wireframes to high-fidelity mockups.

      • Defining the information architecture and navigation structure.
      • Designing the dashboard and core accounting modules.
      • Creating reusable components, forms, and data tables.
      • Structuring transaction-related workflows.
      • Establishing a consistent visual design system.
    3. Client Collaboration:

      I maintained regular communication with the client, an accounting professional, through phone calls. These discussions helped me understand accounting requirements and clarify the information and functionality needed across different modules.

  2. 02 Constraints
    • Worked within the accounting requirements and workflows communicated by the client.
    • Designed around the interconnected nature of financial operations and accounting modules.
    • Completed the design within a two-week timeline.

Problem

01

Complex Financial Information

Accounting systems contain various financial records and reports that need to be presented clearly and remain easy to navigate.

02

Connected Accounting Workflows

Modules like sales, purchases, ledger, banking, and inventory are interconnected, requiring a clear and consistent structure.

03

Data Heavy Interface

The platform relies on tables, numbers, and transaction details, making readability and organized layouts essential.

Design Process

The design process focused on understanding accounting requirements, organizing the product structure, and translating financial workflows into clear interfaces.

01 Analyze

  • Discussed requirements with the client.
  • Studied the core accounting operations required by the application.
  • Referenced Frappe Books to explore established accounting interface patterns.

02 Define

  • Identified the main product modules and their responsibilities.
  • Mapped the information architecture and navigation structure.
  • Defined the interface requirements for financial records and workflows.

03 Ideate

  • Explored dashboard and module layouts.
  • Planned the organization of financial information.
  • Considered navigation, forms, and table structures for common tasks.

04 UI Design

  • Applied typography, color, spacing, and visual hierarchy.
  • Designed wireframes, high-fidelity interfaces and reusable components.

05 Prototype & Refine

  • Created interactive prototypes in Figma.
  • Connected screens to demonstrate key workflows.
  • Reviewed design decisions through discussions with the client.

Design Decisions

Two key design decisions guided the design of the Accounting App: organizing accounting modules and improving the presentation of financial information.

01 Organize Modules Around Accounting Functions

Decision
I aimed to structure the application around its core accounting functions, using a sidebar to provide access to the different modules.
Rationale
Through discussions with the client, I gathered requirements for the accounting workflows and identified the main modules the application needed. I organized these functions into a sidebar to make the different sections accessible within a consistent navigation layout.
Outcome
A structured sidebar navigation that organizes the application's accounting modules and provides direct access to its main functions.

02 Establish Clear Financial Information Hierarchy

Decision
I aimed to organize financial information through clear visual hierarchy, structured layouts, and consistent data presentation.
Rationale
During discussions with the client, I gathered requirements for the application's financial data and accounting workflows. I focused on presenting numerical information, account details, and transaction records in a structured way to make the interface easier to navigate and understand.
Outcome
A structured presentation of financial information across the application's dashboard, tables, and forms.
